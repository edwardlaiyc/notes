+Don’t care conditions

![](images/k-map-01.png)

Grouping 1’s → Sum of minterms

Grouping 0’s → Product of maxterms

For SoM, all ‘1’s must be grouped, each group can have 2x ‘1’s.

To check correct loops, looping 2x elements eliminates x variables.

Loops can wrap around perimeter, and the 4 corners can be looped together too.

Don’t-cares (marked with X’s) can be treated as 1 or 0.

SSI: <12, MSI: 12-99, LSI: 100-9999

Active-high: asserted when logic 1

Active-low: asserted when logic 0
