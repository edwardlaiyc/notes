# Peripherals

- Keyboard & mouse

  - Keyboard can be wired (USB) or wireless (2.4Ghz ISM), detects key presses and transmits characters by ASCII codes.

  - Mouse primarily transmits x,y coords and left/right click.

- Capacitive Touch Interface

  - Touch screen works cause of change in capciitance of the hardware in presence of finger. This change in cap is affected by dielectric of the capacitor.

- Camera

  - Consists of Lens, Image sensors (convert photons to electric signals, then ADC converion for processing), Image signal processor (ISP; auto focus, exposure etc.), Interface I/O (reformatting for delivery to host processor).

- Microphones

  - Use variation in capacitance when diaphragm is displaced by sound pressure to transduce sound wave to electric signals.

  - Transduced signal is analog, but ADC can convert it to digital.

  - ECM microphone: the charges are stored permanently on the electret.

  - MEMs microphone: electrical charges provided by charge pump.

- Liquid Crystal Display (LCD)

  - Liquid crystal molecules twist the light and controls the amount of light passing through the colour filters.

  - The orientation of these molecules controlled by electric field.

- Speaker

  - Electromagnetic coil moves a speaker cone, mechanical energy converted to sound.
