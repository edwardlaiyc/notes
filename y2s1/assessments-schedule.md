- iRA/tRA, 22%
  - Weeks 3, 4, 7, 8, 9 (best 4/5)
  - 5 MCQs each
- Class part, 10%
- Assignment 1, 20%
  - Group, Source Library
  - Deliverables: Word/PDF + NotebookLM
    - A: Source Library Table: 4–5 sources per member, categorised by disciplinary lens (STEM/BIZ/GOV/SSH), spanning multiple disciplines.
    - B: Problem Statement: 200–250 words (prevalence, current literature, the gap).
    - C: Critical AI Reflection: 500–600 words covering Insight, Failure, and Intervention with Gemini Notebook.
- Assignment 2, 25%
  - Group, Poster & Presentation
  - Deliverables: Poster PDF + NotebookLM + oral presentation

    - Oral pitch: max 10 minutes to simulated stakeholders. Not everyone must present, but all must attend.
- Assignment 3, 20%
  - Individual, Reflection
  - Deliverables: Word/PDF + AI Declaration
    - 800 words (±10%)
