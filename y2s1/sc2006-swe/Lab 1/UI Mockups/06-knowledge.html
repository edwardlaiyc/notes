What actually happens to my CPF if I move overseas permanently?
CB

If you give up Singapore citizenship or permanent residence, you may apply to withdraw your CPF savings in full — including your Retirement Account.

If you keep your citizenship or PR and simply live abroad, your CPF stays where it is. It continues to earn interest, and the usual withdrawal rules apply from age 55.

Renouncing citizenship or PR is what unlocks the withdrawal, not the move itself.
