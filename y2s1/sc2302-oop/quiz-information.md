## FAQ for Quiz 2

**Q: Can I write on paper for workings during the quiz?**
A: Yes. Draft paper will be distributed and collected. Please bring your own pen.

**Q: Can I use a calculator?**
A: No.

**Q: Is backtracking allowed?**
A: Yes. However, do not spend too long on one question. All 37 questions carry
equal marks and must be completed within 75 minutes.

**Q: If I finish early, can I leave the lab?**
A: Yes. You may leave after 30 minutes. However, no one may leave in the last
15 minutes to avoid disruption.

**Q: What if I am late?**
A: Students arriving within 10 minutes of the start time may join. After 10
minutes, entry will not be permitted.
